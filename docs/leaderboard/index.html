BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Five ways to understand space.

01 / VIEWPOINT

Camera Rotation

Infer how the camera rotates from synchronized audio and visual cues.

02 / DIRECTION

Source Direction

Identify where a sound comes from, including sources outside the frame.

03 / POSITION

3D Localization

Estimate a sounding source’s position in three-dimensional space.

04 / MOVEMENT

Source Motion

Reason about the heading and motion of a source over time.

05 / SCENE LAYOUT

Cognitive Map

Connect heard directions to positions on a bird’s-eye map.
