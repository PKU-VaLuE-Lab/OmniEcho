02 THE IDEA

A world of sound.
A sense of space.

Sound tells us more than what happened.
It tells us where.

Humans combine what they hear with what they see to make sense of their surroundings. OmniEcho brings this ability to embodied agents: answering spatial questions, locating off-screen sound sources, and navigating toward sounding targets.

Our model pairs a dedicated first-order ambisonics (FOA) spatial encoder with a pretrained semantic audio pathway. Together with vision and language, it connects the meaning of sound to the geometry of the world.
